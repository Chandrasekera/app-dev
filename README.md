**Full Title:** *Cars 3*

**Release Date:** June 16, 2017

**Director:** Brian Fee

**Production:** Pixar Animation Studios

Cars 3 follows the story of **Lightning McQueen**, a legendary racing champion who faces a new generation of faster and more advanced race cars. With the help of Cruz Ramirez, he discovers that being a champion is not just about winning but also about believing in yourself.
